# Build brief: "Claude Commerce Agents, for B2B" hub on growmax.io

## Who this is for
Owners, managing directors, heads of sales and heads of operations at manufacturers and
distributors (industrial, electrical, building materials, spares, MRO). They run on quotes,
price lists, credit terms and reps. They have heard "AI agents will change commerce" and are
asking one question: **what does that mean for a business like mine, this year?**
Write every line for that reader. If a sentence would only make sense to a developer, cut it.

## The story in one breath
Anthropic (the company behind Claude) just published the playbook for how AI agents will
shop and how they will help merchants run their business. That playbook is built for retail.
Growmax ARC brings it to B2B: your pricing, your credit terms, your approvals, your reps,
already in the system, so an AI assistant can quote, order and answer for your business
without ever going around your rules. **Nothing happens without a human saying yes.**

Tone: confident, plain, specific. Short sentences. Real B2B situations. No hype words
("revolutionary", "seamless"), no jargon (no "MCP", "OAuth", "tool registry", "proposal card"
in customer copy). Use the site's existing voice: direct, uppercase display headlines, red
full stops.

Primary CTA everywhere: **Book a Demo → /demo?source=arc-ai-hub**
Secondary CTA: **Join the Copilot early-access list → /demo?source=arc-ai-hub&module=copilot-beta**
(add an "ARC Copilot early access" checkbox to /demo Target Modules; pre-tick when the URL has it)

## Visual system
Reuse the site's existing tokens and blocks exactly: IBM Plex Sans uppercase display, IBM
Plex Mono eyebrows and buttons, ink #0D0D0D on #FAFAFA, red rgb(230,26,26), square corners,
grid-paper hero. Reuse the hero, the "assumes / needs" two-column block, the numbered 01–06
list, the comparison table shell from /arc/compare/*, and the closing Book-a-Demo block.

**CTAs:** Book a Demo · Watch a real session

### Block 1 · "WHAT CHANGES FOR YOU" (three plain outcome tiles)
- **Quotes in minutes, not afternoons.** A rep types "quote Al Baker for 40 cases of salmon,
  their usual terms" and gets a priced, ready-to-send quote. The price comes from your price
  list and your quantity breaks, never from the AI's imagination.
- **Customers served at 9 pm without a phone call.** A buyer asks their assistant "do they
  have NW-1001 in stock, and what's my price?" and gets your answer, for their account only.
- **Nothing moves without a yes.** Every quote, order, stock change or price change is shown
  to a person first. Approve it, or don't. Undo is one click.

### Block 2 · "RETAIL AI ASSUMES. B2B NEEDS." (existing two-column block)
| Retail AI assumes | Your business needs |
| One price for everyone | Customer-specific prices and pack-size breaks |
| Add to cart, pay by card | Quote, approval, purchase order, invoice, Net 30/45/60 |
| Anonymous shoppers | Named accounts with credit limits and a rep who owns them |
| One warehouse | Branches and warehouses that each ship their own orders |
| "Someone else will add the rules" | The rules are already in ARC |
Line under the table: Anthropic's own playbook says the merchant must supply the backend and
the business rules. For B2B, that backend is ARC.

## Page B — /arc/ai/claude-commerce-agents · "Claude Commerce Agents, explained for B2B"
Purpose: the page a sales director finds after reading the news. Explain, then reassure.

**Hero:** `THE AI COMMERCE PLAYBOOK IS PUBLIC. HERE'S WHAT IT MEANS FOR B2B.`
1. **"What Anthropic released"** (5 short lines, plain): a free, open playbook for two kinds of
   AI helper: one that helps a customer find, compare and buy; one that helps the merchant
   watch sales, fix listings, manage stock and prices. It is written for retail. It expects
   the merchant to supply everything underneath: catalog, prices, orders, rules, permissions.
2. **"Why retail's version doesn't fit a distributor"** (the same two-column block as Page A,
   shorter).
3. **"What you get with ARC, mapped to the playbook"** (table shell from /arc/compare/*,
   columns: *The playbook expects you to provide* | *What ARC gives you on day one*).
   Rows in customer words: Your catalog and stock, searchable · Customer-specific prices ·
   Quotes with approval and versions · Orders that respect credit terms · Purchase orders and
   goods receipt · Stock across branches · Returns and credit notes · A human approval step on
   every change · The connection to Claude and ChatGPT · An in-console assistant (early access).
4. **"Use both"**: if your team wants to build on Anthropic's playbook, ARC is the backend it
   plugs into. Your customers' assistants can already read your public catalog.
5. FAQ (with FAQ schema): Is this Anthropic's product? (No. It is Growmax ARC, built in line
   with Anthropic's open playbook.) · Do I need Claude to use ARC? (No.) · Can my customers use
   ChatGPT instead? (Yes.) · What is "early access"? · Can the AI change a price on its own?
   (No. Never without a person approving.)
6. Footnote: Facts about the playbook are from Anthropic's published materials, reviewed
   5 September 2026. Anthropic reports carts up to 35% larger and 60% higher completion for
   retail; those are Anthropic's figures for their retail examples, not ours.

## Appendix A · builder rules (never in customer copy)
- Do not say ARC "runs on Claude". If a model is ever named, say "frontier models".
- Do not use these words in customer-facing text: MCP, OAuth, tool, registry, proposal card,
  state machine, endpoint, schema, token.

## Appendix B · replay player (component spec)
- Static, client-side only. Loads `/replay/index.json` and one `/replay/<scene>.replay.json`
  per tab. No backend. Files arrive later; until then show "Recording coming soon".
- Schema v1: bundle {schemaVersion, lane, sceneId, title, tenant{name,currency}, modelTier,
  recordedAt, totalMs, turns[], cards[], ledger}; turn {turnIndex, user{text,attachments[]},
  events[], summary}; event {t ms, type ∈ token|thinking|step|tool_call|tool_result|card|
  question|usage|done, ...}; card {cardId, kind, statusBefore, statusAfter, payload,
  transitions[{t,status,by}]}.
- Render as a chat: the person's message → the assistant's reply typed at recorded timing
  (4× toggle, skip to end) → small "checking price list…" style activity chips for step /
  tool_call (show a friendly label, never the raw tool name) → a document-style card (quote
  lines, totals in ZAR, status badge that moves Proposed → Approved → Applied) → an "Approved
  by Sudhakar" moment → "Undo available".
- Money as `ZAR 8 250.00`. Show `modelTier` only as Fast / Balanced / High. Never show ids.
- Keyboard operable; with prefers-reduced-motion render the full transcript instantly;
  plain-text transcript toggle.
